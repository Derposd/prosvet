# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want participation in Prosvet to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, political views, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive; assume good faith.
- Focus on facts and measurements, not on people.
- Respect people's safety: never ask contributors to reveal their identity, exact location or IP
  address, and never post such data about others.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, trolling.
- Publishing others' private information (doxxing), including from result files or reports.
- Requests to add circumvention functionality after being told it is out of scope.
- Any conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that violate this code and
may ban contributors temporarily or permanently. Report incidents privately to the maintainers via
GitHub's private vulnerability reporting (Security tab) or by e-mail listed on the maintainers'
profiles. All reports are handled confidentially.
